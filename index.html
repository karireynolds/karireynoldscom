About

I've spent 20+ years sitting between business stakeholders and technical teams — translating ambiguous goals into shipped, adopted systems. For the last few years, that's meant AI: agent workflows, data platforms, and the unglamorous work of getting real production value out of tools that look easy in a demo and are hard inside an enterprise.

Today, as Director of Data & AI at HSO, I own presales scoping and delivery for enterprise AI and data engagements — working directly with C-suite sponsors to define what success looks like, then partnering with data scientists and engineers to build it. Recent work includes AI agents that draft first-pass RFP responses against a client's services catalog and flag contract-risk terms across an NDA repository — the kind of production tooling that has to actually get adopted, not just demoed.

Before HSO, I spent eight years at Slalom leading AI strategy, governance, and technical delivery programs for clients including Salesforce, Portland General Electric, BECU, and AWS — everything from standing up AI Centers of Excellence to owning go-live for multi-use-case deployment programs.

What I'm good at: showing up inside a customer's real environment, figuring out what they actually need versus what they're asking for, and closing the gap between "this works in a pilot" and "this is in production and adopted." I've done it for SharePoint migrations, AI agent deployments, personalization platforms, and enterprise governance programs — always embedded, always accountable for the outcome.

I'm based in Seattle, WA.
