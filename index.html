Portfolio

Two recent builds — small, working tools rather than slideware.

Molt

Multi-agent Gmail cleanup · Claude Sonnet 4 · Gmail + Notion

A five-agent pipeline that clears out promotional inbox clutter. It scans Gmail's Promotions tab, dedupes senders by domain, and automatically processes unsubscribe requests across dozens of senders — flagging anything that needs a manual click, like a mailto-based opt-out, instead of guessing. A summary lands in Notion when it's done.

  1. 1
    Scout — searches Gmail's promotions category for recent emails
  2. 2
    Reader — deduplicates results by sender domain
  3. 3
    Extractor — reads each email and pulls the unsubscribe mechanism (link or mailto address)
  4. 4
    Firer — sends the unsubscribe request automatically for link-based senders, flags mailto-based ones as needing manual action
  5. 5
    Logger — writes a summary report to Notion (counts and a per-sender table)

Revenue Leakage Diagnostic

Conversational diagnostic agent · Claude Sonnet 4 · built for a small business consulting practice

A conversational diagnostic tool, not a static quiz. It interviews business owners one question at a time across eight categories — pricing, no-shows and cancellations, lead follow-up, overhead, cash flow, inventory, labor, and untracked marketing spend — estimating the monthly dollar impact of each gap and recommending a specific fix before moving to the next category. Access is gated to active clients.
